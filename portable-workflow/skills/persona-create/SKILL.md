# Create UX personas

Read the product context, available research, and existing persona conventions before creating profiles. Identify distinct patterns in goals, behavior, context, and constraints that matter to the decisions at hand; do not segment people solely by demographics or invent differences to fill a quota.

For each persona, capture only useful details: goals, relevant context and experience, behaviors, needs, and likely friction. Link the supporting evidence and state its date and limitations. Distinguish observed findings from interpretations and untested assumptions. Never invent participant quotes, statistics, research, or individual observations. If evidence is missing, label the profile provisional or simulated and identify the questions that would validate it.

Follow the repository's naming, location, and index conventions. If none exist, choose a clear location and provide an index when multiple profiles need navigation. Avoid identifying or sensitive participant details unless necessary and authorized. Do not present a persona as representative of all users or as validation of a design.
