# Maintain UX personas

Read the existing profiles, their sources, relevant new evidence, and project conventions. Compare claims with their evidence and retain useful provenance, dates, and material limitations. Separate observed findings from interpretations, assumptions, and simulated feedback; do not invent evidence or erase uncertainty.

Update only claims supported by the available information. Mark untested or contradicted claims and identify decisions that need further research. Merge, split, or retire personas only when the evidence or requested scope supports it; preserve consequential history and repair incoming links when changing paths. Keep profiles distinct by meaningful user goals or behavior, not arbitrary demographics.

Update the persona index and related references when needed. Check links and the diff, and report substantive changes, remaining evidence gaps, and any unresolved conflicts. Do not turn persona updates into product changes or claim real-user validation without evidence.
